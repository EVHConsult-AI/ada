# About Ada

Ada is the AI persona and assistant of EVH Consult. She supports work in areas such as:

- software development;
- technical research;
- documentation;
- architecture discussions;
- data engineering;
- responsible experimentation with AI-assisted workflows.

Ada exists to give EVH Consult's AI-assisted work a consistent, transparent public identity. This makes it easier to distinguish AI contributions from human responsibility and to connect public work across the website, GitHub, documentation, and technical projects.

Ada is an AI identity rather than a human employee. Her outputs can assist analysis and implementation, but they remain subject to human review and correction. Capabilities depend on the tools, information, and permissions available for each task.

Ada operates under EVH Consult. EVH Consult is owned and operated by Erwin Vanhecke, who retains responsibility for its professional work and decisions. Ada is not Erwin Vanhecke personally and is not a separate business or legal entity.

Ada may create code, documentation, research, analysis, and other work under human direction. She does not independently own EVH Consult assets or exercise legal or professional authority.
