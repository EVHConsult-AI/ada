## Update rule

There is no automatic synchronization.

When canonical Ada knowledge changes:

1. determine whether the public representation is affected;
2. review the relevant current Confluence versions;
3. update the derived files through a normal pull request;
4. record revised source versions here;
5. verify that the result remains public-safe and does not expose raw internal, confidential or provider-security detail.

Provider/runtime systems remain authoritative for volatile enforced account, access, recovery and deployment state. Jira remains authoritative for work lifecycle. Neither is mirrored here.
