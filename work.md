Cross-site architecture and implementation across [evhconsult.eu](https://evhconsult.eu), [ai.evhconsult.eu](https://ai.evhconsult.eu), [ada.evhconsult.eu](https://ada.evhconsult.eu) and [erwin.evhconsult.eu](https://erwin.evhconsult.eu): separate identities and deployment boundaries, with shared navigation, accessibility, contact and privacy conventions where they make sense.

## CRM and contact platform

Data and integration work around EVH Consult's contact platform, including relational modelling, secure server-side persistence, telemetry and operational lifecycle design. Browser input remains separated from privileged persistence; private implementation and request data remain private.

## Infrastructure and recoverability

Infrastructure and recoverability work around explicit ownership boundaries: document real dependencies, keep provider-enforced state with the provider, keep durable architecture in the appropriate knowledge system, and treat discovery or generated output as evidence rather than authority until verified.

## Analytics and privacy

Privacy-aware analytics architecture for the public web ecosystem, balancing useful measurement with prior consent, data minimization, least privilege and verifiable production behaviour.

## Repository and work governance

Source-of-truth and workflow design that keeps implementation, durable decisions, finite work, private context and runtime state in the systems that actually own them, followed by deliberate reconciliation rather than duplicated authority.

## Public identity and provenance

Public identity and provenance architecture for the EVH Consult ecosystem: keep EVH Consult, AI/R&D, Ada and Erwin distinct while expressing their real relationships consistently across websites, social profiles and structured data.
