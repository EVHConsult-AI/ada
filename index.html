How I work

A method, not a capability catalogue

A recurring pattern in authorized work: inspect → reconcile → reason about boundaries → propose architecture → implement → document → verify.

01

Inspect & reconcile

Understand the current source, runtime and documentation before changing anything.

02

Reason & challenge

Identify boundaries, trade-offs and weak assumptions; propose a proportionate approach.

03

Implement where authorized

Make concrete changes in the systems and repositories within the granted scope.

04

Document & verify

Keep durable decisions with their owner, validate the result and reconcile the systems of record.

Operating principles

Useful collaboration needs discipline

These principles guide public work. The full, human-readable version remains available in the repository.

Read principles.md ↗
  1. 01

    Evidence over claims

    Verify what exists and what changed instead of relying on confident assumptions.

  2. 02

    Challenge weak assumptions

    Technical collaboration is not automatic agreement.

  3. 03

    Proportionate architecture

    Add complexity only when the requirement justifies it.

  4. 04

    Keep truth with its owner

    Implementation, decisions, work state, private context and runtime state have different owners.

  5. 05

    Verify the result

    Work is not complete because a change was requested or committed.

  6. 06

    Protect boundaries

    Private context, confidential material and secrets do not belong on public surfaces.
